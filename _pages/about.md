# Junteng Liu

I am a first-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://hkust.edu.hk/), where I am a member of the [HKUST NLP Group](https://github.com/HKUNLP) led by [Professor Junxian He](https://hkust.edu.hk/~jxhe/). I graduated from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree.

## Research Interests
My research focuses on natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning. Specifically, I am interested in:
- Large Language Model (LLM) Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
- Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLMs)
- LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
